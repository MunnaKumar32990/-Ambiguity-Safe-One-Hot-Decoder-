Defect Mechanics & Source Citation

scikit-learn #34549
Source Problem:

When OneHotEncoder combines category dropping (drop='first' or drop='if_binary') with handle_unknown='ignore', an all-zeros sub-vector represents both the dropped category and any unseen category.

Vulnerability Impact:

Vanilla inverse_transform silently assigns the dropped category label to unknown/unseen categories with 0% error warning, polluting downstream ML pipelines and decision engines.

Our Ambiguity-Safe Innovation:

Metadata-driven Ambiguity Detector intercepts all-zero collisions at sub-vector granularity, providing 100% recall with four selectable safety policies: Null Withholding, Sentinel Token Injection, Provenance Side-Channels, and Strict Rejection.
